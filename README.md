# ORPHEUS // TE-808 — Interactive World Music History, Direct-Drive Turntable & Playable Web Audio Synthesizer (1151–2026)

**ORPHEUS (`TE-808`)** is an interactive, tactile studio console and world music archive inspired by **Dieter Rams (Braun SK-4)**, **Teenage Engineering**, and **Blue Note / Abbey Road hardware consoles**. Explore **61 landmark compositions & greatest albums of all time (1151–2018)**, drop the needle on a direct-drive turntable to hear real-time synthesized motifs, and play a live **2-Octave Polyphonic Synthesizer & 16-Step Roland TR-808 Drum Machine** directly in your browser.

### 2. CH-02 // Playable Synthesizer & 16-Step Roland TR-808 Rhythm Lab
- **2-Octave Polyphonic Synthesizer Keyboard (`C3 – E5`)**:
  - Play live with your mouse or computer keyboard:
    - **Upper Octave (`C4 – E5`)**: `Q W E R T Y U I O P` (White Keys) & `2 3 5 6 7 9 0` (Black Keys)
    - **Lower Octave (`C3 – B3`)**: `Z X C V B N M` (White Keys) & `S D G H J` (Black Keys)
- **6 Historical Synthesizer / Instrument Voices**:
  1. `1720 Flemish Harpsichord` (Plucked Baroque string synthesis)
  2. `1900 Concert Grand Piano` (Warm hammer-felt harmonic decay)
  3. `1959 Cathedral & Drawbar Organ` (Sustained fifth-drawbar harmonics)
  4. `1970 Minimoog Analog Lead` (Detuned dual-sawtooth + 24dB resonant filter sweep)
  5. `1982 Juno-60 Lush Synth Pad` (Warm slow-attack chorus pad)
  6. `1987 Roland TB-303 Acid Bass` (High-Q liquid resonant squelch)
- **16-Step Roland TR-808 Drum Sequencer**:
  - Program 4 synthesized analog drum voices (`808 Sub Kick`, `808 Snare`, `Closed Hi-Hat`, `Analog Clap`) across 16 steps with live BPM control (`68–168 BPM`) and **5 Iconic Historical Rhythm Presets**:
    - *1982 Planet Rock Electro 808* (Afrika Bambaataa / Kraftwerk)
    - *1970 Funky Drummer Break* (Clyde Stubblefield / James Brown)
    - *1972 Krautrock Motorik 4/4* (Jaki Liebezeit / Can & Neu!)
    - *2006 Detroit Soul Boom-Bap* (J Dilla MPC3000 swing)
    - *1994 London Jungle Break* (160 BPM UK rave cut)
- **Live Web Audio Oscilloscope & Spectrum Analyzer**: Real-time time-domain waveform and frequency bar visualizer built into the synth rack.
